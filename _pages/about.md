I am an M.S. student at Field Robot Lab, Korea University. I work on mobile robot systems where learning, control, and software integration have to be tested before they are moved to hardware.

Most of my recent work is with wheeled-legged platforms, indoor AMRs, and mobile manipulators. The current focus is Isaac Lab/RSL-RL experiments for Go2W-OpenArm KALO, ROS/Autoware integration for indoor delivery robots, shelf-transfer mobile manipulation, and CEBO-based wheel design optimization.
