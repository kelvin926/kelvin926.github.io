I study stability-aware robot learning and autonomy for mobile robotic systems, with work across wheeled-legged loco-manipulation, mobile manipulation, AMR perception/navigation, and simulation-driven robot design optimization. Recent work spans Isaac Lab/RSL-RL/PPO-based Go2W–OpenArm KALO and shelf-transfer mobile manipulation, ROS/Autoware AMR integration with LiDAR-based detection/tracking, and CEBO-based T-pedal wheel design optimization. 휠-레그 loco-manipulation, mobile manipulation, AMR perception/navigation, simulation-driven robot design optimization을 포함하는 모바일 로봇 시스템의 stability-aware robot learning and autonomy를 연구합니다. 최근에는 Isaac Lab/RSL-RL/PPO 기반 Go2W–OpenArm KALO와 shelf-transfer mobile manipulation, ROS/Autoware 기반 AMR 통합 및 LiDAR detection/tracking, CEBO 기반 T-pedal wheel design optimization을 수행했습니다.

KALO: Kangaroo-LocoManipulation

Early-launch assistance for a Go2W–OpenArm wheeled-legged platform, using the manipulator as a constrained inertial appendage during acceleration. Go2W–OpenArm 휠-레그 플랫폼에서 manipulator를 가속 중 제한된 inertial appendage로 사용하는 early-launch assistance 연구입니다.

Role

Tools
Isaac Lab, RSL-RL, PyTorch, PPO, Go2W–OpenArm

CEBO-Based Stair-Climbing Wheel Design

Simulation-driven design optimization for a six-variable T-pedal wheel design problem targeting stair-climbing performance and pitch stability. 계단 등반 성능과 pitch stability를 대상으로 한 6변수 T-pedal wheel 설계 문제의 simulation-driven design optimization 연구입니다.

Role
T-pedal wheel search-space design, CEBO optimization experiments, CoppeliaSim evaluation-loop automationT-pedal wheel 탐색공간 설계, CEBO 최적화 실험, CoppeliaSim 평가 루프 자동화
Tools
Bayesian optimization, CoppeliaSim, Python, CAD
Result
Nominal traversal time decreased by 23.31% (9.405 s → 7.213 s), and body-pitch stability median decreased by 37.84% (1.102 deg → 0.685 deg) under the nominal friction condition.Nominal friction 조건에서 traversal time 23.31% 감소(9.405 s → 7.213 s), body-pitch stability median 37.84% 감소(1.102 deg → 0.685 deg).
Paper
Clustering-enhanced Bayesian optimization (CEBO): A case study on T-pedal wheel design of a stair-climbing robot
