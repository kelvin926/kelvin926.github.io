I am an M.S. student in Mobility Science and Engineering at Korea University, working in Field Robot Lab with Prof. Youngeun Song. My work focuses on wheeled-legged mobile manipulation and indoor AMR systems, with emphasis on stability, evaluation, and implementation. Recent projects include KALO for a Go2W–OpenArm platform, ROS/Autoware integration for indoor delivery robots, a lift-equipped shelf-transfer mobile manipulator, and CEBO-based wheel design optimization.

KALO: Kangaroo-LocoManipulation

Implemented early-launch assistance on a Go2W–OpenArm wheeled-legged platform by using the manipulator as a constrained inertial appendage during acceleration. Built the Isaac Lab/RSL-RL evaluation setup and compared Base, Fixed carrying-pose, and KALO controllers using launch-onset metrics.

Role
Launch-assist concept, Go2W–OpenArm setup, evaluation metrics, Isaac Lab/RSL-RL experiments
Tools
Isaac Lab, RSL-RL, PyTorch, PPO, Go2W–OpenArm
Result
Normalized forward-velocity AUC(0–1.5 s) increased by 15.1% over Fixed; t90 decreased from 1.703 s to 1.067 s; 90%-target-speed trials within 1.5 s increased from 29/128 to 69/128.
Paper
KALO: Kangaroo-LocoManipulation for Early Launch Acceleration in a Wheel-Legged Robot

Lift-Equipped Mobile Manipulator

Designed a lift-equipped mobile manipulator platform for shelf transfer in multi-level rack environments. Built a 9-D action / 41-D observation PPO environment in Isaac Lab for shelf-transfer policy training.

Role
Platform design, 9-D action / 41-D observation Isaac Lab PPO environment, shelf-transfer experiment analysis
Tools
Isaac Lab, PPO, PyTorch, mobile manipulation

CEBO-Based Stair-Climbing Wheel Design

Formulated a six-variable T-pedal wheel design problem for stair-climbing performance and pitch stability. Automated the CoppeliaSim evaluation loop and applied CEBO after validating it on 2D–12D benchmark functions.

Role
T-pedal wheel search-space design, CEBO optimization experiments, CoppeliaSim evaluation-loop automation
Tools
Bayesian optimization, CoppeliaSim, Python, CAD
Result
Nominal traversal time decreased by 23.31% from 9.405 s to 7.213 s, and body-pitch stability median decreased by 37.84% from 1.102 deg to 0.685 deg under the nominal friction condition.
Paper
Clustering-enhanced Bayesian optimization (CEBO): A case study on T-pedal wheel design of a stair-climbing robot
Advanced Engineering Informatics, revised manuscript resubmitted after minor revision
