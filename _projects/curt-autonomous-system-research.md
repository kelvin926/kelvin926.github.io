I worked on a 1/5-scale autonomous-vehicle platform in Korea University's CURT program. I handled ROS-based driving experiments, LiDAR obstacle detection, and camera lane recognition.

- **Role:** platform setup, perception module implementation, autonomous-driving experiment support
- **Tools:** ROS, LiDAR, camera vision, small autonomous vehicle
- **Result:** small-scale autonomous driving setup for perception and control practice.
